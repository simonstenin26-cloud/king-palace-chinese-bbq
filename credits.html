KING PALACE CHINESE BBQ

INFORMATION
& CREDITS.

This website is a design preview created for King Palace Chinese BBQ. It has not been confirmed or endorsed by the restaurant. Business information was researched on September 12, 2026.

Restaurant information

The address, phone number and regular hours are listed by Restaurantji and corroborated by Wanderlog. Hours displayed on the homepage use the America/New_York timezone. Please call the restaurant for holiday hours.

Menu

The homepage contains 38 selected dishes from the published menu listing dated August 25, 2025. It is not the complete current restaurant menu. Amounts are historical listed prices; availability and current prices must be confirmed with the restaurant. Additional menu photographs are available on Restaurantji. Short dish descriptions on this website are editorial summaries, not ingredient or allergen specifications.

Photography

Food images depicting King Palace dishes are remotely embedded from The Infatuation's King Palace review, credited there to Tasty Planet. Their use here is for this design preview; image rights remain with the respective owners. Replace these with restaurant-owned photographs or obtain a license before a public business launch.

Design

Page structure takes inspiration from the CRAV Burgers template supplied for this project. Typography, layout, color palette, crown motif, text treatments, and copy are newly implemented for this preview. The crown and wordmark are proposed design treatments, not an official restaurant logo.

External services

Google Fonts, remotely hosted food photography, and the Google Maps embed connect to third-party services when the page is opened. Call links open your phone app; they do not send an order. There is no checkout, payment collection, reservation form, analytics, or customer-data storage on this site.
